## Brand & Style

The design system embodies modern enterprise logistics precision: authoritative, operational, data-dense, and friction-free. It targets freight forwarders, customs brokers, supply chain directors, and cross-border enterprise traders who handle high-stakes regulatory clearances, freight manifests, and real-time container tracking.

The visual direction merges **Corporate / Modern Enterprise** with **Utilitarian Data Architecture**. It replaces bureaucratic legacy logistics software with an agile, high-clarity command center. Interfaces prioritize scanning efficiency, visual verification speed, and high information throughput while maintaining structural elegance. The emotional resonance is absolute operational reliability, frictionless maritime intelligence, and predictive calm under regulatory pressure.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-frequency international operations in deep, nautical oceanic tones complemented by high-visibility functional signals.

- **Primary Canvas & Chrome (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`)**: The deep maritime navy anchors top navigation, primary controls, key indicators, and authoritative action items.
- **Action & Accent Teal (`#0D9488`, `#14B8A6`, `#06B6D4`)**: Energetic oceanic teal represents autonomous actions, AI-suggested routes, interactive highlights, active tabs, and forward momentum.
- **Surface Neutrals & Structural Slate (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#E2E8F0`, `#CBD5E1`, `#64748B`)**: Clean, low-fatigue slate backgrounds (`#F8FAFC` base canvas, `#FFFFFF` component cards) structured by razor-sharp border dividers (`#E2E8F0`) prevent cognitive overload across dense tables and form layouts.
- **Customs & Trade Compliance Signals**:
  - *Compliant / Released*: Emerald Green (`#059669`, background `#ECFDF5`, border `#A7F3D0`) for cleared customs, verified bills of lading, and clean audits.
  - *Inspection / Advisory*: Amber Gold (`#D97706`, background `#FFFBEB`, border `#FDE68A`) for pending duty assessments, documentary discrepancies, and quarantine holds.
  - *Critical Hold / Non-Compliant*: Crimson Ruby (`#DC2626`, background `#FEF2F2`, border `#FECACA`) for sanctions flags, port detentions, and denied parties.
  - *Tariff & Classification*: Cyan / Indigo accents (`#0284C7`, background `#F0F9FF`) for Harmonized System (HS) automated categorizations.

## Typography

Inter serves as the sole typographic engine across the platform, utilizing its deep OpenType feature set to render logistics data legibly at scale.

- **Tabular Numerics (`tnum`, `cv05`, `zero`)**: Enabled across all financial ledgers, TEU quantities, gross weights (KG/LBS), tariff duties, and timestamps to ensure rigorous column alignment in high-density tables.
- **Incoterms & Code Badges**: Capitalized, slightly tracked (+0.04em to +0.05em) small labels with medium-to-bold weights to separate metadata (e.g., `DDP`, `FOB`, `CIF`, `HS 8471.30`) instantly from descriptions.
- **Hierarchy Structure**: Display titles are reserved strictly for high-level port operations summaries and executive dashboards. Operational workflows operate primarily within `headline-sm`, `body-sm`, and `data-mono` to maximize visible inventory without clutter.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column fluid grid optimized for high-density operations dashboards, split-pane inspection panels, and multi-modal shipment tracking sheets.

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns with a fixed compact sidebar (64px collapsed, 240px expanded), 1.5rem gutters, and 2rem outer viewport padding. Content blocks use fluid spans (e.g., 8-column master manifest list paired with a 4-column AI anomaly inspector).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns with 1rem gutters and 1.5rem outer canvas margins. Master-detail views automatically transition from split-screen to slide-over drawers.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns with 0.75rem gutters and 1rem margins. Complex data grids collapse into stacked status cards prioritizing ETA, customs state, and immediate exceptions.
- **Internal Spacing Cadence**: Component interiors adhere strictly to the 4px baseline rhythm (`space-xs` to `space-md` for compact data cards and dense tables; `space-lg` to `space-xl` for page headers and modal wrappers).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual separation relies on crisp borders and low-diffused, cool-tinted shadows, preserving a sharp, technical aesthetic without distracting realism.

- **Border Hierarchy**: Flat `#E2E8F0` hairline borders (1px) define all card perimeters, table cells, and navigation boundaries. Focused or active structural zones transition to `#0D9488` or `#0F172A`.
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#F8FAFC` base background without elevation.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Data Modules)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px border in `#E2E8F0` and an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Records & Floating Toolbars)**: Slightly elevated with `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` and `#CBD5E1` border brightening.
- **Level 3 (Inspection Drawers & Menus)**: Deep context overlay shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Critical Modals & Sanction Alerts)**: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` with a 40% opacity `#0F172A` backdrop scrim.

## Shapes

The design system uses a strict **Soft (Level 1)** geometry. Enterprise logistics software demands structure, predictability, and maximal content area. 

- Base UI components (inputs, buttons, table rows, cell tags) utilize `0.25rem` (4px) radii.
- Medium containers and cards use `0.375rem` (6px) or `0.5rem` (8px).
- Pill shapes are strictly prohibited except for micro status-dot indicators and circular avatar badges to prevent a casual consumer aesthetic.
- The crisp corners ensure that complex tables, multi-column forms, and nested document trees align to visible vertical and horizontal guidelines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid nautical navy (`#0F172A`) with pure white text, 4px radius, 36px standard height (`space-sm` vertical, `space-md` horizontal padding). Hover state: `#1E293B`. Active: `#020617`.
- **Teal Accent (AI Actions)**: Teal fill (`#0D9488`), white text, used for one-click customs declarations, OCR parsing triggers, and AI route optimizations. Hover: `#0F766E`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#1E293B` text. Hover: `#F8FAFC` background with `#CBD5E1` border.
- **Destructive**: `#FEF2F2` background, 1px `#FECACA` border, `#DC2626` text. Hover: `#FEE2E2`.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Height: 36px (compact data entry). 
- Inactive: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, `#0F172A` text, `#94A3B8` placeholder.
- Focus: 1px `#0D9488` border with a 2px outer glow (`rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`).
- Error: 1px `#DC2626` border with red error caption in `label-sm`.

### Incoterms & HS Code Badges
- **Incoterms (FOB, CIF, EXW, DDP)**: 20px height, 4px radius, uppercase `code-badge` typography. Crisp tinted background: Navy Neutral (`#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text, `#E2E8F0` border) or Teal Special (`#CCFBF1` background, `#0F766E` text, `#99F6E4` border).
- **HS Code Tags**: Monospaced tabular numeral presentation with copy-on-click action, subtle cyan tint (`#F0F9FF` background, `#0369A1` text, `#BAE6FD` border).
- **Customs Verification Pills**:
  - *Cleared*: `#ECFDF5` background, `#047857` text, with a 6px solid emerald dot.
  - *Review Needed*: `#FFFBEB` background, `#B45309` text, with a 6px solid amber dot.
  - *Customs Hold*: `#FEF2F2` background, `#B91C1C` text, with a 6px solid red dot.

### Data Tables (Logistics Manifests)
- Border-collapsed styling with 1px `#E2E8F0` horizontal dividers.
- Row heights: 40px compact operational density. Alternating row hover: `#F8FAFC`.
- Column headers: `#F8FAFC` background, `label-sm` uppercase styling in `#64748B`, with sticky positioning during scrolling.
- Numbers, currencies, container IDs (e.g., `MSCU1234567`), and weights formatted using `data-mono` tabular figures.

### Cards & Detail Modules
- Clean `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `0.5rem` radius.
- Card Header: 44px height, integrated bottom hairline border (`#F1F5F9`), containing title in `headline-sm` alongside quick-action toolbars.
- AI Insights Card: Border highlighted with a left 3px solid teal accent (`#0D9488`) on an ultra-light tint (`#F0FDFA`) to distinguish autonomous logistics recommendations from raw user data.
